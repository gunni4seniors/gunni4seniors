Park Activities

Photos by: Melba Hogue, Elizabeth Gillis & Stuart Schneider

Arts & Crafts

Tap into your creative side with our friendly arts and crafts gatherings! Whether you're into sewing, knitting, painting, or simply trying something new, everyone is welcome. It’s a relaxed, enjoyable way to make something special while connecting with neighbors and new friends.

Live Music & Dancing

Enjoy evenings filled with music, laughter, and movement. We host occasional concerts along with line dancing taught by Deborah. Whether you want to tap your toes, join the dancing, or simply enjoy the moment, our music nights bring the whole park together. Check with your host or the office for upcoming dates.

Holiday Activities

Holidays at Palisade RV Park are full of celebration and community spirit! From summer BBQs and potlucks to music and games, we make every holiday feel warm and memorable. Bring your appetite, bring your smiles, and join us for seasonal fun under the Colorado sky.

Sunday Worship

Start your Sunday with a peaceful, uplifting worship service. Everyone is welcome to join, whether you’re visiting for a weekend or staying for the season. It’s a warm space for reflection, encouragement, and connection with others.

Food at the Park

Good food brings people together! Join us for potluck brunches, happy hours, and community meals where neighbors share dishes, stories, and great company. It’s an easygoing way to meet others and enjoy the welcoming atmosphere of the park.

Jeep Outings

Ready for adventure? Join fellow campers for Camper Organized, but not sponsored by the Park jeep outings on scenic mountain passes and trails of all difficulty levels. Whether you’re chasing views or seeking a thrilling ride, there's always a friendly crew ready to explore the beauty of the Gunnison Valley together.

Cards & Board Games

Take a break from screens and jump into classic fun! From cards and puzzles to a variety of board games, our game gatherings offer a friendly space for conversation, connection, and a little lighthearted competition. All skill levels welcome!

Chair Exercise Class

Stay active with our gentle chair-based exercise sessions! These classes focus on flexibility, balance, and movement that feels good for all ability levels. It’s a great way to keep your body energized and enjoy a supportive wellness community.

The Mission of the Gunnison Home Association (GHA) is to assist in the provision of basic needs and amenities to seniors in the Gunnison Valley. These services may include housing, food assistance, transportation, medical needs, or social and cultural enrichment. Through our activities and grant program, we aim to enhance the lives of senior citizens in our area—whether full-time or seasonal residents.
